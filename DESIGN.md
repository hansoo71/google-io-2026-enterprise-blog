## Applied design principles
- Stark white canvas and black ink.
- Serif, oversized, compressed editorial headline hierarchy.
- Hairline rules, black section dividers, and newspaper-like grids.
- Minimal chrome; no rounded SaaS cards as primary layout language.
- Sparse electric-blue accent for metadata and data highlights.
- Responsive conversion from dense editorial grid to stacked mobile reading flow.

## Logo rule
No WIRED logo, wordmark, masthead image, or brand asset is used. `FIELD NOTE` is a generic local publication label for the report.
